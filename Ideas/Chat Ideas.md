## Current direction — 2026-10-13

**Priority update — 2026-10-14:** Focus discussion on actual UI and gameplay before environment maps and wider visual polish. See idea 005. This chat remains vault-only; game implementation requires a separate request.

**Navigation decision — 2026-10-14:** Keep **Explore** for PvE worlds, solo/co-op travel, discoveries, and world bosses. Keep **Battle** for PvP challenges and any later ranked mode. This supersedes the proposed Worlds/Arena renames in idea 008.

### Cats and carriers

- Buy different types of cat carriers from a little store at spawn.
- Open the carriers at spawn to receive cats.
- Carriers contain **cats only**. Toys, furniture, currencies, and other rewards are not carrier contents.
- Buy toys separately for the sanctuary.
- Carrier choices are based on the power of the cats, rather than the earlier suggested elemental themes.
- Cats have different rarities, and different rarities have different powers.
- Exact carrier names, prices, contents, probabilities, rarity tiers, and power values are not decided yet.

### Spawn

Spawn is the shared social and activity area:

- A hangout place.
- A place to trade.
- The little carrier store and carrier-opening area.
- A boss fight in the middle of spawn.
- Leaderboards showing different things from the game.

### Server boss event

- The boss fight is a server event in the middle of spawn.
- The event has prizes.
- Boss appearance, timing, participation rules, difficulty, and prize distribution are not decided yet.
- A proposed rotation, health table, and prize rules are recorded in idea 002 below; they are not final balancing decisions.

### Leaderboards

- Show different achievements or measures from the game.
- Exact categories and whether they are server-wide or global remain undecided.
- Proposed categories and scoring rules are recorded in idea 002 below.

- Players can see their cats there.
- Players can buy toys and other things for their cats.

## 002 — Trading, server bosses, and leaderboards

**Recorded:** 2026-10-13  
**Status:** PROPOSED DESIGN — ideas and reusable mockup prompts only.  
**Scope:** Expand the three systems explicitly requested by the user. Keep carriers cat-only, spawn social, the boss central, and the named special-cat hierarchy intact. All numbers below are proposed starting values, not current game data or approved balance.

### A. Trading at spawn

Trading takes place between two players in the spawn hangout. Select a nearby player and choose **Request Trade**. They can accept or decline; ignoring the request expires it. Include a **Trade requests: On / Friends only / Off** setting and a short request cooldown to prevent spam.

For this first concept, trade owned cats directly. Do not introduce an auction house, a separate trade currency, or toys inside carriers. Trading unopened carriers or currencies is not part of this proposal.

#### Offer and confirmation flow

1. Open a two-sided window: **Your Offer** and **Their Offer**. Both sides browse their owned cats and add the cats they want to exchange.
2. Each offered cat card shows its portrait, name, rarity, power, and any carried upgrade investment. Each side also shows the sum of offered power. Power is information, not a claim that the trade has equal value; rarity and personal preference matter too.
3. Favorite-locked cats cannot be added until their owner explicitly unlocks them. Equipped or actively assigned cats must be made available first. Trading should never silently remove a cat from an active activity.
4. Each player presses **Ready**. Any addition/removal clears both Ready states.
5. Once both are ready, show a final summary of exactly what each person gives and receives, followed by a five-second review countdown.
6. After the countdown, both players independently press **Confirm Trade**. Changing the offer returns to the first stage and clears all confirmations.
7. The exchange completes together, or neither side receives/spends anything. Show **Trade complete** and the received cat cards. Cancellation, disconnection before completion, or a missing cat ends the trade with no exchange.

Suggested initial capacity: up to four cat entries per side. Melman & Julian are one entry, shown together on one card and never split. A one-way gift can use the same flow, but the giving player's final summary must clearly say **You receive no cats**.

IVY and the duo can be tradable under this proposal, with an extra named-cat warning in the final summary. Owner-only/nontradable copies, if ever introduced, must be labeled and rejected by the trade flow; their existence is not assumed here. Account names/Usernames should be visible alongside DisplayNames to reduce mistaken identity.

If implemented later, the server must verify ownership and availability, prevent the same cat appearing in two simultaneous exchanges, preserve the cat's identity/upgrades, and save an identifiable trade record. A client animation or Ready button cannot itself transfer ownership.

### B. The boss fight in the middle of spawn

The center of spawn is a circular boss arena. The carrier store, trading area, and leaderboards remain around its outside. Players opt in at the arena boundary; people hanging out or trading outside are unaffected by boss attacks.

During the advance warning, players select their cats and enter the ring. The proposed boss mode is a shared fight: participating cats attack the same boss health pool, while trainers reposition and dodge clearly marked attacks. This is a new encounter proposal, not a claim that the existing battle system already supports real-time cooperative fights.

Use three understandable boss actions: **Pounce** with a marked landing circle, **Paw Sweep** with a marked arc, and **Stomp** with an expanding ring. Give visible windup time before damage. A participant knocked out returns to the arena entrance and can rejoin; this should not delete cats or spend currency. Avoid making one player's mistake damage uninvolved people at spawn.

#### Rotation and timing

Proposed schedule: one event starts every 15 minutes, following a four-boss cycle that repeats each hour. The schedule is consistent across servers, but each server fights its own boss with its own participants and health pool.

| Start time, minute within each hour | Boss | Visual identity | Base health for four participants | Victory Fish Coins per eligible player | Guaranteed cat-only carrier |
|---|---|---|---:|---:|---|
| :00 | Big Tabby | Oversized orange tabby | 30,000 HP | 100 | Tier 1 Carrier |
| :15 | Tuxedo Captain | Oversized black-and-white tuxedo | 60,000 HP | 200 | Tier 2 Carrier |
| :30 | Maine Coon Guardian | Oversized fluffy Maine Coon | 90,000 HP | 350 | Tier 3 Carrier |
| :45 | Royal Calico | Oversized ordinary calico, clearly distinct from IVY | 120,000 HP | 500 | Tier 4 Carrier |

“Tier 1–4” are carrier placeholders based on cat power; their final names, cat pools, and odds remain undecided. The bosses are encounter characters, not new collectible cats or prizes. IVY remains the rarest and strongest collectible, with Melman & Julian below her. Encounter HP is not a collectible cat-power ranking.

- **One minute before the start:** show the next boss, countdown, and Join Event prompt.
- **At the start:** boss appears; begin a six-minute fight timer.
- **After victory:** show a 30-second reward/results panel, then restore the quiet central arena.
- **If six minutes expires:** the boss leaves, show a failure result, and resume the countdown to the next scheduled event. Do not delay the entire cycle because one server loses.
- Show the next boss and time until its scheduled start when no fight is active.

#### Health scaling

Count players who opted in during the warning and are ready at the start. Set health once:

`Boss HP = base health × starting participant count ÷ 4`

With no participants, skip that event. One player gets one quarter of the table health; eight get twice the table health. Do not scale with nonparticipants hanging out at spawn. Late entrants can help without increasing the health already shown; treat this as a cooperative advantage and tune it later if necessary.

For a sizing illustration only, assume each participating player's selected cats collectively deal **100 damage per second** while attacking. Four players then deal 400 DPS: the table bosses take about 75, 150, 225, and 300 seconds respectively before dodging or interruptions. The displayed cat power-to-damage relationship has not been designed yet; 100 DPS is not an implemented formula. Real health and rewards require playtesting against the eventual power curve.

#### Eligibility and prize distribution

Give the table's full coin reward and one carrier to **every eligible participant** when the server wins. Do not divide one carrier among the server or reserve the main reward for the last hit.

Suggested eligibility: a player lands at least three valid attacks and is actively participating near the end of the fight. Weak cats qualify through activity, without a percentage-of-total-damage threshold. If the whole fight ends in under 15 seconds, one valid attack is enough. A player who never joins, idles without participating, or leaves the event early does not qualify. Support-based eligibility can be added only if actual support actions become part of the design.

Late entrants can qualify under the same rules. A brief disconnect after qualifying should preserve entitlement if the player was active during the final 30 seconds; a disconnect should not duplicate a payout on rejoining.

Small damage bonuses provide recognition without taking the base reward away:

| Eligible damage rank in that fight | Extra Fish Coins |
|---|---:|
| First | +10% of that boss's base coin reward |
| Second | +5% |
| Third | +3% |
| Everyone else | No extra coins; full base reward still granted |

Round fractional coins down. Equal damage shares the same rank/bonus, using competition ranking (two first-place players mean the next player is third). No bonus carriers or exclusive named cats depend on damage rank. Rank only damage actually removed from the boss; overkill does not count.

Example: winning Royal Calico grants every eligible player **500 Fish Coins + one Tier 4 Carrier**. First place receives 550 coins; second receives 525; third receives 515. All carriers contain cats only.

On timeout, active eligible participants receive **25% of the base coins**, rounded down, but no victory carrier and no boss-win credit. For Royal Calico, that is 125 coins. This softens a failed attempt while keeping victory valuable.

Rewards should be saved automatically. The results panel is a receipt with **Continue**, not a Claim button that can accidentally forfeit earned prizes. Each player can earn one result per scheduled event slot, including across reconnects/server hopping; victory and failure rewards cannot both be claimed for the same slot. Future implementation must track that entitlement centrally.

IVY and Melman & Julian are **not included in these ordinary boss prize pools**. Their acquisition remains undecided; this avoids inventing a common source for the rarest cats.

### C. Leaderboards and their exact criteria

Place a clear leaderboard display outside the boss ring. Use separate category tabs instead of mixing unrelated stats into one unexplained score. Include **This Server / Global**, and **All Time / This Week** where suitable. A small pinned **Your Rank** row lets a player see progress even outside the top ten.

| Category | Ranking criterion | Period |
|---|---|---|
| Team Power | Sum of the player's strongest owned cats that fit the permitted team-slot limit; Melman & Julian occupy one slot | Current snapshot |
| Cat Collection | Number of distinct cat entries discovered at least once; duplicates do not increase the count, the duo is one entry, IVY is one entry | All time |
| Boss Wins | Number of victorious boss events for which the player qualified for rewards | All time and this week |
| Boss Damage | Total effective damage dealt in victorious, reward-eligible boss events; exclude overkill and failed-fight farming | All time and this week |
| Battle Trophies | Current trophy balance earned through the game's player-battle system | Current snapshot |
| Fish Coins Earned | Cumulative coins awarded through normal gameplay, including boss prizes; spending does not reduce this value | All time and this week |

Team Power uses the strongest legal owned combination, so switching a favorite into the active team does not unexpectedly drop leaderboard rank. Exact team capacity and power calculation still need a separate decision. Trading away a cat changes current Team Power, while its historical Collection discovery remains.

The earned-coins category excludes admin grants, paid grants, refunds, and money transferred between players. The trading proposal above exchanges cats, so no coin trading is assumed. Owner/developer-granted cats should carry provenance and their boosted contributions be excluded from competitive Team Power and collection discovery scores unless those entries are later acquired normally. Admin/test boss results should also be excluded. Exact provenance rules are a future implementation detail, not a claim about the current data model.

For current-stat boards, equal values share a rank. For count/total boards, equal values also share a rank; use a stable account ordering only to arrange their rows, not to give one tied player a better rank. Use competition ranking consistently (1, 1, 3). Weekly earned-stat boards reset at **Monday 00:00 UTC**, with the reset countdown visible. Lifetime discoveries/totals and current ownership are unaffected by that weekly reset. These boards award recognition only; no leaderboard prizes are proposed here.

## 007 — UI/gameplay improvements and new feature options

**Recorded:** 2026-10-14  
**Status:** ASSISTANT SUGGESTIONS — not approved implementation or additions to the confirmed feature set.

### Original request

> give me a few other ideas for improvements of what we already have and new things

These suggestions build on the recorded cat collection, carrier/merge routes, cooperative worlds, and UI/gameplay-first priority. They do not assert that the proposed world/multiplayer systems already exist. Real-time-specific options depend on the eventual combat decision.

### Improvements to the existing concepts

1. **Saved team loadouts.** Save a few named teams such as Solo, Boss, and Friends. Show the three slots, total power, and each cat's move summary; switching a saved team should be easy before a run. Melman & Julian remain one slot. Validate actual ownership and availability when loading a team, and explain missing/traded cats rather than silently substituting. The appeal is spending less time repeatedly equipping the same cats.

2. **A visible collection goal.** Pin a desired regular cat from the Catpedia. Its panel shows which carrier can grant it and the exact merge recipe, with owned/missing inputs. Include a Keep for recipe marker to help protect ingredients from accidental trades/merges. Show actual odds when defined; do not imply a random carrier guarantees the pinned result. This turns a huge collection into an understandable next goal. Special cats show only their actual decided acquisition route, not an invented ordinary recipe.

3. **A party-ready screen.** Before traveling, show each trainer, their selected cats, the world difficulty, scaling, and personal reward rules. Everyone presses Ready; changing the selected world/team clears readiness. Use an invitation/join shortcut from the normal player list, and show which friend is still choosing. The aim is fewer confusing starts and clearer expectations, not a new social currency or mandatory party gate.

4. **Useful run results.** Show what the player earned, which collection/upgrade goal advanced, any new world unlock, and a clear next action: Play Again, Try Next World, or Return to Spawn. A carrier stays unopened until the player chooses to open it. Party replay requires the other players' readiness, and rewards save independently of the results screen. Avoid drowning the player in overlapping reward popups.

### New gameplay options

5. **Temporary choices during a run.** At a checkpoint, each player can choose one small benefit for that run, such as a stronger guard, quicker ability recovery, or better recovery-item effectiveness if those systems exist. Benefits expire when the run ends, do not replace permanent cat progression, and do not create a new currency. Use one or two choices per run, bounded values, and a short/default selection so one player cannot stall the party. This adds replay variety to a familiar route.

6. **Boss opening windows.** A clearly signaled boss attack leaves a brief recovery window when avoided or countered correctly. Players time abilities together for a satisfying burst. Make the opening readable and achievable solo too; no boss requires bringing a friend. This is an optional real-time mechanic, with exact triggers/bonuses undecided, and should not erase the required power hierarchy of IVY and the duo.

7. **Optional side objectives.** A short rescue/escort of a carrier, an optional guarded cache, or a marked tougher encounter can appear along a route. The party chooses a quick run or an extra challenge for more personal rewards. Keep the task brief, mark the reward before committing, and respect individual loot entitlement. Avoid building a large quest system before the core battles work; the carrier still contains only cats.

8. **Try a cat's moves in Practice.** A Practice option on a cat's details lets the player try attacks, ability timing, switching, and evasion against a simple target. Display what the moves do and make cooldowns readable. No entry fee, ranked score, or farmable rewards. This helps players understand a new cat and gives the future UI/combat prototype a small, focused testing space before large worlds are built.

### Suggested priority

Start discussion with **saved teams, a pinned collection goal, and useful run results**: these make the core battle/collect/upgrade loop easier to understand. The party-ready screen becomes important when co-op travel is designed. Add one replay-variety or boss mechanic after the basic combat feels good, rather than adopting all eight ideas automatically.
