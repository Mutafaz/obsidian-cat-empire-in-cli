# Cat Catalog - 1000 Concepts

**1,000 collection entries: 998 regular designs + Melman & Julian (one entry) + IVY.**

Written concepts only, linked from [[Chat Ideas]]. No game catalog, assets, models, or saved player data were changed.

These are collectible game designs, not a claim that 1,000 real cat breeds exist. Regular entries combine 50 breed-inspired/design families with 20 coat-pattern briefs; two candidate combinations are omitted to reserve the two special entries. Some stylized combinations would not be conventional purebred coloring. Family names guide future silhouettes; they are not biological registrations. The list contains named variants rather than 1,000 unrelated species.

Type here means the cat family/design identity, not an elemental combat type. Every entry remains a cat. Any later combat-type mapping is a separate decision.

Visual briefs specify proposed coat palettes, markings, and eyes. Hairless/fine-coat designs should express coat patterns as skin markings as appropriate. Family silhouettes and real reference details must be reviewed during a separately authorized asset task; no appearance is verified here.

Power values are illustrative collection power scores, not production rates, currency values, or boss DPS. At equivalent upgrade investment, higher regular rarity is stronger; IVY is above the duo and the duo above every regular cat. Upgrade caps and the power-to-damage formula remain undecided.

## Acquisition rules

- Buy the listed carrier at the little spawn store and open it at spawn. Carrier prices and drop probabilities are not decided. Carriers contain cats only.
- Every regular entry also has a specific deterministic merge recipe below. Select the desired result, supply those owned cat entries, and preview the result before confirming. IDs identify catalog types; actual consumed inventory instances must be selected separately.
- Common recipes exchange two other Common cats for the selected Common design. This is a collection-completion option, not guaranteed power growth.
- Uncommon uses three Common inputs; Rare uses three Uncommon; Epic uses three Rare; Legendary uses three Epic; Mythic uses three Legendary. All named inputs are consumed only when the selected result is successfully granted.
- This is a proposed recipe system, replacing the earlier assumption of same-species/equal-level merging only if later authorized. The current source has not been changed.
- Favorited, equipped, assigned, trade-reserved, or otherwise unavailable inventory instances cannot be silently consumed. Preview invested upgrades and any refunds/carryover before confirming; the exact upgrade policy remains open.
- The ordinary carrier and merge pools exclude IVY and Melman & Julian. They cannot be ordinary recipe ingredients. Their special acquisition route is not decided; do not invent odds or consume them to make other cats.
- A carrier draw grants one cat entry. The duo, if later available through a special carrier, would still be one entry/slot and depict both cats.
